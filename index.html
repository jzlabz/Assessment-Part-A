About Me

My Biography

My name is Jethro Ebereiye, also known as Zani or Dr JZ, I'm a 27-year-old Nigerian with a background in healthcare, a growing passion for technology,and a strong desire to build solutions that make everyday life easier.

I am a licensed Pharmacist in Nigeria, a graduate of the University of Benin (UNIBEN), and someone who is constantly looking for ways to learn, grow, and explore new opportunities.

At the core of who I am is curiosity. I enjoy understanding how things work, learning new skills, and finding practical ways to apply what I know to solve problems. My journey so far has taken me from studying Pharmacy to developing a serious interest in software engineering, and I see these two fields as complementary rather than separate.

Education and Academic Background

My academic journey took me to the University of Benin, where I studied Pharmacy and developed a strong foundation in pharmaceutical sciences, healthcare, and patient care.

I successfully completed my degree with a CGPA of 4.05, an achievement that reflects the dedication, discipline, and consistency I put into my studies.

Studying Pharmacy taught me more than just the science behind medications. It exposed me to the importance of accuracy, critical thinking, responsibility, and, most importantly, the human side of healthcare.

After completing my studies and fulfilling the necessary requirements, I became a licensed Pharmacist in Nigeria. My professional background in healthcare continues to influence many of the things I want to accomplish in technology.
